Returns

You can return any part within 30 days if it hasn't been fitted and is in its original packaging. Start a return from your order history or send us a message with the order number, and we'll e-mail a prepaid label.

If we sent a part that doesn't fit the car you told us about, we pay the return shipping and send the right part first. Electrical parts that have been connected, and fluids that have been opened, can't be returned for safety reasons unless they are faulty.

Privacy Policy

We keep your name, contact details, delivery address, saved car and order history so we can process orders and check fitment. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, and we never see or store your full card number.

We only send marketing e-mails if you subscribe, and every e-mail has an unsubscribe link. You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time through the contact page.

Terms & Conditions

Prices include the parts shown and exclude delivery unless stated. Fitment information comes from the vehicle maker's catalogues; we check it carefully, but please compare the part number with the old part before fitting.

Safety-critical parts such as brakes, steering and suspension should be fitted by a qualified mechanic. Our liability is limited to the price of the part supplied.
