About Rawstuf

An independent parts counter that checks the part against the car before it ships.

We started behind a trade counter

Most of us spent years on a parts counter, handing brake pads and filters across to mechanics who knew exactly what they needed. Online, nobody was doing that check. People ordered by photo, waited three days, and found the part was for the facelift model.

So Rawstuf works the way a good counter does. You tell us the car once. Every part on the site is matched to the maker's catalogue by year, make and model, and an advisor looks at your order again before it is packed. If something doesn't line up, we call you before we ship it, not after.

  • 40,000+parts on the shelf or one day away
  • 300models in the fitment catalogue
  • 30 daysto return anything unfitted
